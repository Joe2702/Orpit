# Store listing

Everything Play Console asks for under **Grow → Store presence → Main store
listing**, in English (the default) and Arabic (add it under *Manage
translations → Add your own translations → Arabic*). Paste it as it is. Each
limit is Play's; every field here is under it.

The images are in this folder:

| Field | File | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| App icon | `icon-512.png` | 512 × 512 |
| Feature graphic | `en/feature-graphic.png`, `ar/feature-graphic.png` | 1024 × 500 |
| Phone screenshots (upload in order) | `en/screenshot-1-home.png` … `en/screenshot-6-review.png`, and the same names in `ar/` | 1080 × 1920 |

They come from the web demo's sample data, so no real person's money is in
them.

## English (en-US)

**App name** (30 max)

```
Orbit: Habits, Money & Sleep
```

**Short description** (80 max)

```
Habits, workouts, sleep and money. Spending tracked from your bank SMS.
```

**Full description** (4,000 max)

```
Orbit keeps your habits, training, sleep and money in one place.

Turn on bank messages and Orbit tracks your spending for you. Every card payment, transfer and salary alert from your bank or mobile wallet becomes a transaction in the right account, with the balance your bank reported. The messages are read on your phone, and their text never leaves it.

MONEY
• Spending from bank and wallet messages, or added by hand in a few taps
• Safe to spend: what's left this month after the bills still to come, and what that is per day
• Budgets that warn you before you go over, not after
• Subscriptions and bills spotted in your payments, with a reminder before each one
• Goals that follow a real account, and your net worth across all of them
• Several currencies, converted at the day's rate

HABITS
• Weekly targets: a week counts at 70%, so one slow day doesn't break your run
• Plan when and where each habit happens
• Counters for anything you want to tally, and milestones to look back on

TRAINING AND SLEEP
• Log workouts with sets, distance and time
• Track sleep: hours, bedtime and how rested you felt

SEE HOW IT ADDS UP
• Week, month and year views. Tap any bar for what's behind it
• A weekly review: one minute to look back, and one thing to aim for next
• Home-screen widgets

ACCOUNTABILITY PARTNER (optional, 18+)
• Pair up with someone and keep each other going
• Share only the trackers you pick. Everything starts private

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• No ads, no third-party analytics, and your data is never sold
• Lock Orbit with your fingerprint or phone PIN
• Export everything, or delete your account at any time

In English and Arabic, with a full right-to-left layout.
```

**Release notes** for the first build (500 max)

```
The first release: habits, workouts, sleep and money in one app, with spending tracked from your bank messages. In English and Arabic.
```

```
يجمع Orbit عاداتك وتمارينك ونومك وأموالك في مكان واحد.

فعّل رسائل البنك ويتتبّع Orbit إنفاقك بدلًا منك. كل دفعة بالبطاقة وكل تحويل وكل إشعار براتبك من بنكك أو محفظتك يتحول إلى معاملة في الحساب الصحيح، مع الرصيد الذي أبلغ عنه البنك. تُقرأ الرسائل على هاتفك، ولا يغادره نصها أبدًا.

المال
• الإنفاق من رسائل البنوك والمحافظ، أو تضيفه بنفسك في لمسات قليلة

• ميزانيات تنبّهك قبل أن تتجاوزها، لا بعدها

• أهداف مرتبطة بحساب حقيقي، وصافي ثروتك عبر كل حساباتك
• عملات متعددة تُحوَّل بسعر اليوم

العادات

التمارين والنوم
• سجّل تمارينك بالمجموعات والمسافة والوقت

شاهد كيف يتراكم كل شيء

شريك الالتزام (اختياري، لمن هم 18 عامًا فأكثر)

خاص من البداية

• صدّر كل بياناتك، أو احذف حسابك في أي وقت

بالعربية والإنجليزية، مع واجهة كاملة من اليمين إلى اليسار.
```

## Store settings

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| App or game | App |
| Category | Productivity (Finance also fits; Productivity is the closer match for habits + money together) |
| Tags | Habit tracker, Budgeting, Personal finance, Sleep tracking, Workout tracking (pick what Play offers) |
| Contact email | youssif_mohammed@aucegypt.edu |
| Website | optional; leave empty or use the privacy page's site |

The names of banks and wallets are left out on purpose: Play treats other
companies' names in a listing as a trademark question, and "bank and wallet
messages" says the same thing without raising it.
